Dual engine

Parakeet v3 for fast English dictation, Whisper large-v3-turbo for 99 languages including Hindi and Hinglish. Picked automatically by language.

Smart formats

Emails, websites, times, dates and amounts land written, not spelled out. "Five thirty pm" becomes 5:30 PM.

It learns your words

Correct a name once and SwarScribe learns it into your personal dictionary. Your file, your words.

Code Mode

"Snake case user name equals five" becomes user_name = 5. Dictate code without fighting the engine.

Meeting Mode

A whole meeting becomes summary, decisions and action items. No bot joins your call.

Private by design

No account, no upload, no training on your voice. Wispr Flow is cloud only and caps its free plan. SwarScribe is neither.
