Why not Wispr Flow?

Wispr is cloud only and caps its free plan. FlowScribe runs on your machine, has no word cap, works on Linux, speaks code, turns meetings into notes, and costs nothing.

Dual engine

Parakeet v3 for fast English dictation, Whisper large-v3-turbo for 99 languages including Hindi and Hinglish. The pipeline picks the right one automatically.

Private by design

No account, no upload, no training on your voice. Your dictionary, snippets and history are plain files you own.
